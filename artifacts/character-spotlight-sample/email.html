basic

Agile Attack

Highest available level 7

Attack primary target for 270% damage + Chain to 2 adjacent enemies for 270% damage.

Gain Evade.
If this character already had Evade, gain Speed Up.

special

Web Slinger

Highest available level 7

Attack primary target for 360% damage + clear 3 positive effects + apply Defense Down for 2 turns and Slow for 2 turns.

Chain to 4 adjacent enemies for 360% damage + clear 3 positive effects + apply Defense Down 2 turns and Slow for 2 turns.

This attack is Unavoidable, cannot be counterattacked, and this attack can Chain to Stealth targets.

Tangled Web

Highest available level 7

Attack primary target for 500% damage.

With 3 HERO allies, reduce Safeguard by 1 on primary target.
With 3 AMAZING AVENGERS, reduce Safeguard by 2 instead.

Clear all positive effects on the primary target + apply Stun and Slow.
